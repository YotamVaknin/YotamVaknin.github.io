Yotam Vaknin

PhD student, The Hebrew University of Jerusalem
QEC researcher, Q-Factor

I’m a theoretical physicist working where device physics meets quantum error correction. I’ve developed physical protocols for both superconducting qubits and neutral-atom arrays, as well as state-of-the-art QEC constructions. More broadly, I think about reducing the overhead of quantum computation across the entire stack.
